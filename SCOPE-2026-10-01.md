## THRML extension (2026-10-02)

THRML is an explicitly selected experimental backend, installed through the
ordinary `thrml==0.1.4` wheel. Renderer ownership covers strict model mapping,
resource admission, generated programs and native sampling. Executor/analysis
ownership covers bounded readiness, fresh-result binding and statistical result
validation. Shared integration ownership covers registry, configuration,
CLI/API/MCP and release gates. Independent review is required across these seams.

Acceptance requires actual THRML conditional sampling compared with an independent
NumPy forward/backward oracle, explicit tensor orientation and retained samples;
independent component and bounded factored/modality composition; rejected malformed,
over-budget and unsupported models; real cancellation/child cleanup; current-run
execution-ID/script-SHA binding; configuration precedence; ordinary installed GNN
wheels outside the checkout on both supported JAX splits; readable analysis; and
complete documentation/API/registry discovery. Gibbs approximation, empirical
uncertainty and CPU/JAX execution must not be reported as exact inference,
convergence, policy optimization, hardware energy or speed acceptance.
